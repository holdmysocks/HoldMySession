# HoldMySession 0.10.6

- **Auto fit for the map's layout editor.** While editing the layout, press **Auto fit** and every session is arranged to fill the screen. Each session keeps its forks and subagents under it, sessions on the same branch sit next to each other, and what does not fit in a row goes on a new row below, so it is easy to manage instead of one long row. You can still drag cards afterwards, and Reset layout still works.
- Includes everything in 0.10.5 (active time in Stats, and the "vs before" fix). If you are coming from 0.10.4 or earlier, update every linked machine together.

Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
