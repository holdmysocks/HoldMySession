# HoldMySession 0.10.4

Far less disk and memory use on a machine with a large history, which matters most on a master.

- **The history scan no longer re-reads every transcript.** It used to read all of them in full at every scan, even when nothing had changed, and hold them all in memory. On a large history that meant gigabytes of disk reads and gigabytes of memory, again and again. It now keeps a small digest of each session and reads a transcript again only when it changed, or when one of its subagents' transcripts did. In a test on 1.3 GB of transcripts, a scan with nothing changed went from reading 1.2 GB in 6.3 seconds to 67 MB in 1.4 seconds.
- **The first scan after updating reads everything once** to make the digests, so expect that one to be heavy on a very large history. After that, only what changed is read.
- **The history is the same as before.** A request that sits in more than one session, as in a fork, is still counted once.
- **The license is now the GPL-3.0.** The source is at https://github.com/holdmysocks/HoldMySession, and the downloads carry the license text.
