# HoldMySession 0.8.6

The desktop app now tells you when a session needs you, says what it wants, and Stats compares with the period before and looks ahead to the end of the month.

## Needs you

- **Alerts.** With the Needs you hook on, the desktop app shows a notification the moment a session starts waiting on you, and puts a count on the tray (menu bar) icon, with an amber dot on the icon itself. Clicking the notification opens the Map on that session. There is no alert while the window is in front of you. Settings, General has a switch.
- **What it is waiting for.** A waiting session now says what it wants, instead of "Waiting on you": "Wants to run Bash", the question it asked, "The plan is ready to approve", or "Waiting for your next message". It shows on the Map (banner, list, selected card and the card itself), on the Sessions rows and in the alert.

## Stats

- **Against the period before.** For every range except All, the headline chips and the four totals show the change against the period of the same length just before it, such as "-22% vs the 7 days before".
- **This month.** What the month has cost so far at API prices, where it is heading from the finished days, what your plan costs, and how many times over the plan is paying for itself at that pace. The first days of a month say it is too early to project.
- **When you start sessions follows the range** and the project you pick, instead of always showing all history.

## Also
