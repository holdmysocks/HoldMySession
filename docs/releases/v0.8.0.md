# HoldMySession 0.8.0

A new look, and Stats that counts each day exactly.

**Update every linked machine to 0.8.0.** The history database has a new table, so a node and its master refuse each other until both run this version. A node on 0.7.0 that has automatic updates on will fetch it by itself.

## The new look

- **Eight themes**, with Dusk (dark) as the default: Dusk, Ink, Dracula, Nord, Clay, Sage, Alucard and Solar. Pick one in Settings, General. A theme changes the whole app, background and all. If you had picked the old light theme, you get Clay.
- **A top bar** replaces the sidebar: the menu in the middle, the project and machine pickers and the gear on the right. On a phone the menu floats at the bottom.
- **Stats, Sessions, Map and Settings** are all rebuilt, and each works on a phone.

## Stats

- **Headline.** Total tokens, the cost at API prices, what you spent on your plan, and chips for this week, how many times what you paid, and what you saved.
- **Range switch** (7d, 30d, 45d, 90d, 180d, 1y, All). It changes the totals, the chart, the rhythm grid and the models. It starts on 45d.
- **A stacked chart** of the top three models and the rest as Other, **Your rhythm** for the last 26 weeks, model shares, and **When you start sessions** with the busiest hours marked.
- **Projects** are listed with their tokens, sessions, API price and last activity. Click one to see the whole page for just that project, with a live thumbnail of its map and its latest sessions.
- **Exact days.** The history database now keeps each day's messages and tool calls. A session counts on the day it started, and its messages on the days they happened. **Time with Claude** follows the range, and every total shows what this week added. A session whose transcript is gone keeps its totals, spread over the days it ran. The next scan after updating fills this in by itself.
- Without the history database, open time per day is worked out from the session files instead.

## Sessions

- Ruled rows with a status word: **NEEDS YOU**, **RUNNING**, **DONE** or **IDLE**.
- The filter has **Running**, **Needs you** and **Done**. The header shows how many sessions there are, how many are running and how many need you.

## Map

- Cards in four distinct colours: green running, amber needs you, indigo done, grey idle.
- When sessions need you, an amber banner on the map and a list underneath, longest wait first, with the chosen session in a card beside it and a button to the next one.
- On a phone the map is a list, with subagents indented under their sessions.

## Settings

Flat rows with pill switches, the plan as a segmented choice with its history, and a ruled pricing table.

Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
